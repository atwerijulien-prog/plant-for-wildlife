Plant today. Wildlife thrives tomorrow.

Plant for Wildlife LBG is a community-led conservation group in Duasidan, Ghana. We plant trees, restore habitats and teach young people to look after the land they live on.

Join our work Contact us

Protecting nature. Restoring wildlife. Empowering communities.

Our mission is to restore and protect natural ecosystems and to raise awareness of conservation in local communities. We work through tree planting, habitat restoration, wildlife conservation, environmental education and community engagement.

We believe that protecting wildlife and fighting climate change go hand in hand. When local people, especially young people, become stewards of their environment, the change lasts for both people and nature.

Together, we can plant today for a greener tomorrow, with wildlife and communities thriving side by side.

What we do

Tree planting

Native trees planted with community volunteers to rebuild forest cover and lock away carbon.

Habitat restoration

Repairing degraded land so animals have food, shelter and safe routes through the landscape.

Wildlife conservation

Protecting local species and teaching neighbours how to live alongside them.

Environmental education

Lessons and field days that show children and young adults how ecosystems work.

Community action

Local people lead the projects, so the work belongs to the community and keeps going.
